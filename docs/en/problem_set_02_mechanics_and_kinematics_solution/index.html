Course Philosophy

This course is not about solving lists of tasks, but about building your own understanding of physics through systematic work with problems. The repository is your personal textbook — a record of how you analyze physical models, interpret results, and organize knowledge. Tasks serve as tools for thinking: their goal is not to obtain a number, but to develop a reasoning process you can reproduce independently.
